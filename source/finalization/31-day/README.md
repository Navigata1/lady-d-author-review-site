# Thirty-One Mornings of Light

Production-proof package for Susan "Lady D" Damon's 31-day visual devotional.

## What is included

- A 31-day content corpus: ten selected readings from each trilogy volume plus a new Day 31 culmination.
- A 95-page, 6x9 print proof. Each day has a visual page, devotional page, and Carry It With You response page.
- A full-screen motion edition with local Three.js assets and a complete devotional reader.
- A 31-card scene review console with the approved prompt architecture.
- Directional movement art and one Day 31 creative-direction draft.

## Production boundary

The content and product architecture pass their current audits. The final visual lane does not. All 31 daily scenes must be generated at print resolution, score at least 88, clear the hard-reject rules, and receive author approval before the print or motion editions are called visually final.

Open `lady-d-31-day-scene-review.html` to review prompts and candidate status. Open `lady-d-31-day-motion.html` for the living edition. The PDF is the portable print proof.
