Root cause, visible on inspection: the sunrise/sky strip is genuinely warm and bright, but it's a narrow band in the upper-center of the frame. The flanking hillsides, grasses, and foreground path shoulders are rendered in near-black shadow silhouette on both sides — easily 50–60% of the frame area. On a bookshelf thumbnail, that shadow mass is what the eye reads first. That is precisely “they just look dark... they're deserts.”
03 · The existing prompt packs

Where these covers actually came from

Ten-concept cover prompt packs already exist per volume (Codex / Claude / Leonardo variants), living at lady-d-author-review-site/downloads/*-10-Cover-Prompts.md. Every concept in a volume shares one fixed palette line — and that palette is the second half of the problem.

VolumePalette as writtenSymbol bank
I · Surrendering to God's Lovedeep indigo, warm cream, sunrise gold, soft rose, earthy umberopen hands, golden sunrise, warm mantle, quiet path, fatherly shelter

Every palette leads or ends on a genuinely dark hue (umber, midnight, teal, violet shadow) with nothing in the brief telling the model how much of the frame that dark hue is allowed to occupy. The model defaults to using it for the entire lower half. Lady D's complaint is the predictable result, not a fluke of one bad generation.
04 · Her brighter reference

What she actually sent as the direction she wants

Two images shared in this working session (not saved to disk as files, so reproduced here as precise descriptions rather than embeds). These are the clearest positive instruction Lady D has given — brighter, warmer, and far more illustrative than anything in the prompt packs above.

Her own mockup · sent as “something along this line”

“Radiant Surrender” wraparound

  • Full golden-hour valley, sun low and warm, sky in bright peach-to-amber — not a narrow strip, the light fills most of the frame
  • A woman in a flowing white dress seated on a rock, viewed from behind, facing the sunrise
  • A bald eagle in flight over the valley, wings spread, catching the light
  • Wildflowers in the foreground — gold, white, soft rose — sunlit, not silhouetted
  • Rolling green hills and a distant river, lit rather than shadowed
  • Ornate gold script title treatment: “Surrendering to GOD'S LOVE!” with a red heart accent
  • Wine-red spine with gold lettering; back cover carries a devotional poem in her own words and an ISBN barcode block
This is a materially different production approach from the prompt-pack photoreal-landscape direction — it reads as illustrated/painterly rather than photographic, and it explicitly includes a human figure, which the existing prompt packs' shared “avoid-list” (no plastic faces, no cheap stock-photo worship poses) was written to steer away from. Both directions are legitimate; they are not the same brief.
Second reference · an alternate attempt she also reacted to

Ornate title-forward variant

  • Similar golden valley/sunrise base, brighter and more saturated than the shipped covers
  • Larger, more decorative gold script for the title, taking up more of the frame
  • Less negative space reserved for subtitle/author typography than a retail-standard KDP layout would want
05 · Corrected brief, per volume

What to actually build

Each volume below keeps everything she is on record as liking (the path, the footsteps, the sunrise/mountain feeling, the existing symbol bank) and fixes exactly what she flagged: a hard brightness floor, and a cap on how much of the frame any dark hue may occupy. A second, illustrated direction follows her own mockup for volumes where a human figure fits the theme.

Volume I — Surrendering to God's Love

God the Father
kept: golden sunrisekept: quiet pathkept: open handskept: warm mantlenew: eagle (from her reference)new: wildflowers, sunlit not silhouetted
Brightness floorAverage frame luminance ≥ 165/255. No shadow mass may exceed 25% of frame area.
Palettesunrise gold, warm cream, soft rose, sage green — drop “deep indigo” and “earthy umber” as dominant hues; indigo may appear only as a thin accent at the extreme top edge.
Light ruleThe sun/light source must illuminate the ground plane and foreground flora directly — no silhouetted hillsides framing the path.

Book cover for 'Surrendering to God's Love' by Susan 'Lady D' Damon. Subtitle: 'A 365-Day Devotional Journey into the Father's Heart'. A sunlit dirt path winds through a bright wildflower meadow toward a warm, wide-open golden sunrise — the light fills at least two-thirds of the frame. Wildflowers (gold, cream, soft rose) and grasses along the path are sunlit and glowing, NOT in shadow or silhouette. No dark hillside mass framing either side. Palette: sunrise gold, warm cream, soft rose, sage green, a thin line of soft indigo only at the very top edge. Open hands motif optional, catching the light. Premium Christian devotional, warm, mature, radiant, hopeful — brighter and more open than a moody landscape painting. Portrait 2:3, spine-safe margins, KDP thumbnail legible, no text, no watermark, no plastic faces, no extra limbs.

06 · What changes downstream

This brief now governs the Codex generation handoff

Lady-D-Cover-Codex-Handoff.md qualification gate has been updated with a mandatory brightness check — average frame luminance ≥ 160/255 and no single shadow mass over 25% of frame area — measured the same way as Section 02 above, so a cover cannot pass qualification while repeating this exact defect. The ten directions in the handoff should be regenerated against the corrected specs above before the next generation pass.
