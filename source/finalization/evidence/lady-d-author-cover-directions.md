# Lady D Author Cover Directions

## Evidence Boundary

This brief distinguishes Lady D's recorded feedback from the production team's
interpretation. It is the source of truth for the August 2026 cover generation
round. The artwork remains a review candidate until Lady D selects a direction.

## Lady D's Recorded Feedback

> "I see that it's just, it's just a little dark."

> "If they were all sitting on the shelf, they just look dark... Almost like the
> framing that they hold as a darkness. They're not bright... you know, they're
> deserts..."

> "They need to be higher quality to me... The depth and the meaning of what is up
> here [is good]. And I like the Jesus one. You can see the footsteps in the thing
> as well... it is nice."

Her emailed Volume I example adds the most concrete visual direction:

- A luminous golden-hour valley and peach-to-amber sky.
- A Black woman in a flowing white dress, viewed from behind, seated on a rock
  and facing the sunrise.
- A bald eagle in flight with its wings catching the light.
- Sunlit wildflowers in gold, white, and soft rose.
- An ornate warm-gold title treatment with a small red-heart accent.
- A wine-red spine with gold lettering.
- Her poem on the back cover, with an ISBN/barcode zone.

## Shared Trilogy Direction

The trilogy should feel like one spiritual journey across a bright shelf: the
Father receives, the Son leads, and the Spirit fills. Light is the connective
device. Strategic foreground shadow may create depth, but it must support the
light and may not dominate the frame.

All generated artwork is 2:3 portrait, full-bleed, without baked-in words,
logos, borders, mockup devices, or watermarks. Typography is applied after Lady
D chooses the image family.

## Volume I: Surrendering to God's Love

### Author-grounded visual lane

A radiant golden valley, open landscape, peaceful surrender, woman in white,
eagle, wildflowers, and a warm sunrise. The image should feel cherished and
safe, not passive or sentimental.

### Variations

1. **Her Golden Valley** - the emailed composition, refined with cinematic
   depth and an uncluttered title zone.
2. **The Path Into the Father's Light** - a bright meadow path leading toward a
   broad sunrise, with the woman and eagle smaller in the composition.
3. **Held Above the Valley** - a more intimate foreground of the woman in white
   and flowers, opening into a vast bright horizon.

Palette: sunrise gold, cream, soft rose, fresh sage, peach, and a thin soft-blue
upper-sky edge. Average luminance target: at least 165/255.

## Volume II: Walking with Jesus

### Author-grounded visual lane

A warm road through sunlit olive groves or open fields, unmistakable sandal
footprints in the path, and a bright dawn ahead. Lady D explicitly praised the
footsteps, so every variation retains them. The cover should communicate
companionship and daily discipleship without depicting a generic stock portrait
of Jesus.

### Variations

1. **Footsteps at Dawn** - a close foreground trail of sandal prints leading
   toward warm light.
2. **The Olive Road** - a luminous path under green-gold olive branches, with
   the prints catching the sun.
3. **Beside the Open Field** - footprints along a bright field road, with a
   distant, non-identifiable companion silhouette used only if it remains
   reverent and anatomically natural.
4. **Carried on the Way** - a Sea of Galilee-era shoreline path. The lower half
   shows two complete walkers' trails side by side; each trail alternates left
   and right first-century sandal impressions. At the halfway point, one whole
   trail stops suddenly. One complete alternating trail continues toward a
   distant man in a simple period tunic carrying another adult safely in his
   arms. This fuses the daily-walk theme with the central revelation of the
   familiar Footprints poem without reproducing any existing illustration.

Production language is deliberately precise: "one pair" means one walker's
ongoing left-right trail, not two isolated sole marks. The transition happens
at the middle of the image rather than gradually blending the two trails.

Palette: dawn gold, parchment, clean white, sunlit olive green, and a thin
midnight-blue rim. Average luminance target: at least 160/255.

Historical reference note: first-century BCE/CE sandals preserved at Masada and
other Judean Desert sites use simple flat leather soles and narrow straps. The
cover therefore uses elongated sole impressions with restrained strap marks,
not bare toes or modern athletic tread.

## Volume III: Filled with the Holy Spirit

### Author-grounded visual lane

Brilliant sunbeams break through parting clouds over a rain-fresh green meadow.
A white dove and a gentle flame are the brightest points. Wind, fresh water,
and movement may deepen the image, but receding storm elements stay at the
outer edges.

### Variations

1. **Dove in the Opening Sky** - a white dove crosses radiant beams over a
   refreshed meadow, with a small warm flame below.
2. **Wind Over the Field** - grasses and wildflowers move in a visible current
   of light, with dove and flame forming a clear spiritual center.
3. **After the Rain** - reflective water, fresh green life, a brilliant opening
   in the clouds, and the dove/flame pair carried by light rather than darkness.

Palette: flame gold, dove white, sunlit sky blue, fresh rain-washed green, and
deep teal only at the extreme upper edge. Average luminance target: at least
160/255.

## Qualification Gate

- Average luminance is at least the volume target.
- Dark mass occupies no more than 25 percent of the image.
- The image reads clearly at a 300-pixel retail thumbnail.
- The title zone remains calm and legible after typographic overlay.
- Human figures, hands, wings, feathers, and footprints are anatomically sound.
- No baked-in text, pseudo-lettering, signature, watermark, or ISBN.
- Each volume is distinct, while palette, light direction, realism, and finish
  make all three recognizable as one family.
