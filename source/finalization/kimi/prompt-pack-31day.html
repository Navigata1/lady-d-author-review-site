Section 01 · The Design System

One System, Thirty-One Skies.

Four rules hold every morning together, no matter how far the environments wander — from fjord mist to firefly meadow. If a generated frame breaks one of these, it does not ship.

Rule 01

Composition

  • The woman is always seen from behind — identification without likeness — placed in the lower third, small against grandeur.
  • Rule of thirds: horizon and figure share the lower two bands; the upper 40% stays brush-block-safe negative space for type.
  • Full-bleed HDR nature or wonder background; portrait 9:16 framing, generated at 4K, social-friendly at first glance.
  • One scene, one light condition, one weather per day — never a collage.

Rule 02

Type

  • Pinyon Script carries the day title — large, emotional, ≥ 90pt at the 1080×1350 working size.
  • Fraunces carries the scripture excerpt — 40–48pt, tight leading, roman or italic as the day’s type mood directs.
  • Inter carries labels: DAY 10 · DEPTHS, references, affirmations — 600 weight, letterspaced, uppercase.
  • Never more than three voices on one card; script and serif do the feeling, Inter does the filing.

Rule 03

Blocks

  • Brush-stroke chips hold the words, with a torn-edge mask and 18px inner padding — never hard rectangles.
  • Royal purple rgba(93,63,130,.92) holds the DAY label and the scripture excerpt, set in linen.
  • Cream rgba(246,239,226,.94) holds the closing affirmation, set in ink.
  • Blocks sit inside the negative space the prompt reserves — never over the figure’s face line or the light source.

Rule 04

Brand Thread

One purple thread runs through all thirty-one mornings — lavender into royal — with sunrise gold as the accent that means “light arriving.” Each day’s palette below ends with the brand chip so the set reads as one family.

Section 04 · How to Run This Pack

From Spec to Keepsake.

Three steps, in order. The prompts are final-draft quality: they already carry the woman-from-behind composition, the brush-block-safe negative space, and the no-text guards.

1

Approve or adjust any day

Lady D reviews titles, scriptures, and palettes first. A day is locked only after she approves it — swap a verse or a scene here, on paper, before anything is generated.

2

Generate the 31 visuals

Run each copy-ready prompt verbatim at 9:16 portrait, 4K, HDR. Keep the upper negative space clean for the brush blocks, and regenerate any frame that adds text, watermark, or a visible face.

3

Assemble keepsake + social set

Compose the day label, scripture, and affirmation over each visual using the Type and Blocks rules, then export the keepsake PDF and the social set. The motion notes become the app layer in a later session.
