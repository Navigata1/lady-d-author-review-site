01

Phase 1 · Scripture re-architecture

The Correlative Weave.

The problem is honest and simple: the current drafts walk the Bible nearly in order — Genesis toward Revelation like a reading plan. Faithful, but mechanical. The fix is not randomness; it is relationship. Passages are chosen because they complete each other across the canon, so a tired reader on a Tuesday in March meets the same promise from two different ends of the Book. This phase turns that instinct into a repeatable engine.

01

Keep the registries

The 366-entry registries per volume stay exactly as audited — day, title, theme, month. Titles and monthly themes are locked. Only the scriptures move, and they move on purpose.

02

Re-seat by theme, not canon order

For each monthly theme, draw passages from at least four of the five biblical regions, so no month ever feels like a single-shelf library:

Torah & History Wisdom & Psalms Prophets Gospels Epistles & Revelation
03

Anchor + Thread, every day

Every day receives one anchor passage plus one correlative “thread” passage from a different region that illuminates the same promise. The connection is explained in one or two sentences in The Thread block — never implied, always taught.

04

Build the glossary while reseating

Every assignment is logged passage → day → page in biblical order, Genesis to Revelation, so the back-matter scripture glossary writes itself as the weave is woven — no second pass, no orphaned references.

Phase 2 · Voice & depth

Seven Passes, One Voice.

No page ships because it was written; it ships because it survived all seven passes. Each pass has one job, changes one layer, and leaves one kind of proof behind — so when a page reaches Pass 7, nobody wonders whether it is ready. They can see it.

The 7-pass refinement — run per volume, in order, no skipping
PassFocusWhat changesWhat proof it leaves
1 Structure lock The fused journal-prompt format becomes final — “Today’s step” and “Morning impact” live inside the prompt as one unit, not three fragments. A signed format spec; every page in the volume maps to it without exception.
2 Depth expansion Bodies grow to roughly 350–450 words. Each day opens with the Father, Son, or Spirit revealing something about Himself before asking anything of the reader, and lands one memorable sentence a tired reader can carry all day. A word-count report per day; the takeaway sentence underlined in the review copy. The takeaway is felt, not just found.
3 Lady D voice filter Warm, direct, motherly, Jesus-centered. Second person. Every day ends in a short prayer plus a reflection — a voice that sits beside the reader, never above her. A read-aloud pass on the samples; Lady D approves 10 sample days before the filter runs volume-wide.
4 Theology & translation proof Every quotation checked word-for-word against the chosen NKJV or KJV text; every original-language lens checked for testament fit and restraint. A clean diff with zero unresolved flags; citation list matches the glossary log.
5 Mature-eye readability Type size, line length, and breath tuned for the reader who will actually hold this book — generous margins, short paragraphs, nothing cramped. A printed proof page read at arm’s length without a squint; 11.5–12.5pt serif confirmed in the layout file.
6 Glossary, TOC & front matter The weave’s log flows into the back-matter glossary (biblical order, day and page), the table of contents, and the front matter in one motion. The glossary matches the audit day-for-day; every TOC entry resolves to a real page.
7 Lock + checksum The manuscript freezes. Any later edit formally reopens the pass it touches — no silent changes after lock. A checksum and a dated lock note in the project record; the dashboard gate flips to source-frozen.

Voice principle

Warm & motherly

Steady, hopeful, practical — the encourager who has walked through the storm herself and left a lamp burning for the next traveler. Never academic, never generic.

Voice principle

Jesus at the center

Every page turns toward Him. Volume themes may feature the Father or the Spirit, but the road always rounds to Christ — His words, His compassion, His invitation.

Voice principle

Direct & practical

Second person, plain speech, one small action a real morning can hold. The reader is never lectured; she is accompanied, and then gently set walking.

Voice principle

The takeaway is felt

Each day lands a single sentence worth carrying — the kind that gets copied onto a note card, taped to a mirror, or sent to a daughter. If a day lacks one, Pass 2 sends it back.

“You are not behind, dear one — you are being led.”

Phase 4 · Companion journals

A Journal That Travels With the Book.

The journals are not notebooks with a logo; they are the second half of the conversation. The devotional speaks; the journal listens back. Each of the three companions mirrors its devotional day-for-day, in the fused format the July 6 meeting settled.

Each day · 1

Scripture echo

A short reprise of the day’s anchor — a verse or phrase, not a reprint — so the journal opens the same door the devotional just walked through.

Each day · 2

Two to three questions

Journal questions in Lady D’s voice that carry “Today’s step” and “Morning impact” inside them — reflection and action fused, never a tacked-on homework block.

Each day · 3

One small action

A single doable gesture for the day — call her, forgive that, walk outside and thank Him for one thing. Small enough to finish before the coffee cools.

Each day · 4

A short prayer

Two or three lines, first person, warm — the page closes the loop so the reader ends in conversation, not in conclusion.

Design details

Lined space that feels designed, not default — hairline rules with breathing room, a small accent mark per month, and nothing fighting the pen. A monthly divider matches the devotional’s accent so the two books move through the year together. The intro page, “How to travel with this journal,” is written in Lady D’s voice: part welcome, part permission slip, part quiet instructions from a friend who has done this before.

Acceptance criteria

  • Every journal day mirrors its devotional day — 366 for 366, leap days included.
  • The fused format holds on every page: echo, questions, action, prayer — nothing amputated.
  • Twelve monthly dividers per journal, accents matched to the devotional.
  • The intro page is approved by Lady D before layout locks.

Phase 7 · Future foundations

Design For It. Don’t Build It Yet.

These lanes are real, and they are later. Each one gets exactly enough attention now to guarantee that nothing we ship this year stands in its way — and not one hour more. Scope creep dies in this section, politely and permanently.

Foundation

The audiobook lane

Lady D — or a collaborator — narrating, with voice-filter experiments later. The 7-pass manuscripts are already narration-shaped: second person, short sentences, daily units. When the time comes, the script falls out of the books for free.

Foundation

The 31-day app

Parallax layers, a day-unlock rhythm, journal input — the prompt pack is already app-shaped by design (Phase 5). The build is a future engagement with its own intake; the foundation is this year’s free gift to it.

Foundation

Translations

The Correlative Weave is translation-aware by construction — themes, threads, and registries reseat cleanly in Spanish and beyond. A translation is a reseat, not a rewrite, because the architecture was never English-only.

Foundation

Workshops & seminars

The trilogy teaches well in a room: the weave, the journals, the 31-day rhythm. A workshop package is a real product — scoped separately, priced separately, after the trilogy ships.

Risk register + cadence

Gates Over Dates. Every Week Has a Shape.

The plan does not pretend nothing can go wrong; it decides in advance who owns each risk, what trips it, and what happens next. Then it wraps the whole effort in a weekly rhythm small enough to keep and strong enough to matter.

Risk register — owner, trigger, response
RiskOwnerTriggerResponse
Payment link drifts Jon The $1,400 link is not live inside the week. Every button routes to the pending status page; escalate at the Friday touchpoint until cleared.
Reseat breaks uniqueness Production Audit shows a passage over 3 uses, or any duplicate. Re-run the weave for that month only; the glossary regenerates from the log — never patched by hand.
Voice drift across 1,098 entries Lady D + Production Sample days read generic, academic, or out of character. Re-apply Pass 3 to the affected month; Lady D re-approves 10 days before the filter resumes.
