## Daily Devotional Contract

1. Print the full Scripture text on every daily page, not only the reference.
2. Use KJV for the reader edition so the complete trilogy can display Scripture without exceeding the NKJV gratis-use ceiling.
3. Keep an original-language note only when it clarifies the printed verse. Place it in the context note, not in the devotional body.
4. Build the year as twelve thematic journeys. Do not present the source references as a mechanical Bible-order crawl.
5. Use a second Scripture only when a human reviewer confirms that the connection is contextually faithful and materially strengthens the reading. A strong single passage is better than a forced pairing.
6. Give the devotional enough body to develop one clear spiritual movement and end with a memorable, day-specific send-off.
7. Preserve Lady D's warm, direct, Jesus-centered, practical, motherly, and preacherly voice. Favor relationship over performance and conviction without shame.
8. Remove separate `Today step` and `Morning impact` blocks. Fuse practical action and the journal question under `Reflect and respond`.
9. Keep the written prayer.
10. Use dates without weekdays or a production year so the books remain evergreen. Preserve seventh-day Sabbath theology only where the passage or theme calls for it.
11. End each devotional with a biblical-order Scripture journey index showing reading date, day number, title, primary passage, and final print page when pagination is locked.
