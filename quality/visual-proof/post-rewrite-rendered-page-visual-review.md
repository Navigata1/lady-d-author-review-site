## Findings

- All reviewed pages use the intended 6 x 9 portrait frame with stable margins and no visible clipping.
- Daily pages clearly separate date/day, title, full KJV Scripture, Scripture context, devotional body, closing line, prayer, and reflection/response.
- Long titles wrap without colliding with Scripture or running heads.
- The corrected Volume 1 Day 8 prayer reads naturally and contains no weekday artifact.
- The corrected Volume 1 Day 112 reading contains no detached seventh-day statement.
- Journal pages provide usable prompts and sustained writing space; monthly review pages preserve the same visual system.
- Scripture Journey Index pages remain readable, ordered by biblical reference, and include date, day, title, and final page.
- The three interiors are visually coordinated while retaining distinct Father, Son, and Holy Spirit accent colors.
- No reviewed page showed blank output, overlapping text, missing glyphs, broken rules, or visibly truncated content.
- The frozen-build recheck confirmed that the corrected titles and prose fit cleanly, the Volume 2 journal's diversified prayer-record and follow-through prompts remain usable, and the final index remains legible after regeneration.
- The three longest-title pages wrap in a balanced two- or three-line block without clipping, collision, margin intrusion, or displacement of the required devotional sections.
- The repaired Scripture-invitation sentence and `teach me to rest in` prayer constructions appear correctly in the regenerated PDFs and preserve the intended one-reading-per-page composition.
- Volume 2 Day 187 now reads as a complete 107-word passage-specific body without the redundant deepening scaffold, and the regenerated page remains balanced and unclipped.

## Boundary

This PASS supports the local rendered-proof gate only. It does not replace:

- author approval of the final manuscripts and proof PDFs;
- Amazon KDP Previewer review for margins, bleed, pagination, and print warnings;
- review and approval of a physical proof copy;
- final exterior-wrap approval after authoritative KDP spine dimensions are known.

Public/KDP release status therefore remains **HOLD**.
