## Editorial Decisions

- Day 2: retain Exodus 15:4; replace invented timing, militarized fear caricature, and watery slogans with remembered deliverance, named inward struggles, faithful action, and praise before resolution. This changes the generated entry, never the author-written evidence.
- Days 5, 6, 11: replace vague final exhortations with truth-telling and repair, bounded responsibility, and teachable service.
- Day 13: small numbers are not the basis of God's choice; do not turn smallness into a new achievement requirement.
- Day 20: Pharaoh's welcome is not a guarantee of every listener's response; honesty does not require indiscriminate disclosure.
- Day 26: lodging does not establish that Jacob slept peacefully or did nothing all night.
- Day 30: a returning memory does not revoke mercy; practical support and repair replace a guarantee that fears cannot return.
- Day 41: remove the invented claim that a lamb was already walking up the mountain. Keep the question, trust, and provision in their proper narrative relationship.
- Day 43: company honors grief without making it painless; ask for a specific, attainable form of help.
