# Surrendering to God's Love: Companion Journal

## A guided journal for a 365-day devotional journey into the father's heart

Susan "Lady D" Damon

Use this journal beside the devotional. Return to the printed Scripture, tell the truth before God, and leave each page with one clear response. The prompts are intentionally direct so the journal supports the devotion rather than repeating it.

---

# January: Loved Before You Perform

Receive belonging before the day asks anything from you.

## January 1 | Day 001

### Questions That Belong Inside the Promise

**Return to the Word:** Genesis 15:2 (KJV)

> And Abram said, Lord GOD, what wilt thou give me, seeing I go childless, and the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus?

**Observe:** What word, action, promise, warning, or picture stands out in Genesis 15:2?

**Reflect:** What honest question have I been afraid to bring to God because I thought it made my faith look weak?
