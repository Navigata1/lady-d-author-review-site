Day 01

Wonderfully Made

Before the world named your flaws, God called His workmanship wonderful. Let His voice be the loudest one in the room today.

I am fearfully and wonderfully made

Psalm 139:14 KJV

PrayerFather, quiet every cruel voice in me. Teach me to see what You see and walk today as one deeply loved. Amen.

I agree with God's love.

You Are Not Alone

Fear may say the road is yours to survive. God answers with His presence: I am with you, even here.

Fear thou not; for I am with thee

Isaiah 41:10 KJV

PrayerFather, meet me in the place that feels too large. Steady my heart with the nearness that never leaves. Amen.

I do not walk alone.

He Mends What Hurts

The broken place is not where God turns away. It is where His gentle hands begin their careful work.

He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.

Psalm 147:3 KJV

PrayerHealer, touch the ache I cannot explain. Bind what is torn and teach my heart to trust Your tenderness. Amen.

My wound is held with care.

Be Still Here

You do not have to solve everything in this moment. Let stillness make room for the God who is already here.

Be still, and know that I am God

Psalm 46:10 KJV

PrayerGod, quiet the noise within me. Help me release what I cannot control and recognize Your presence now. Amen.

Stillness is not abandonment.

Deuteronomy 31:8 KJV

PrayerLord, go before my fear. Give me grace for the next step and trust for what I cannot see. Amen.

Psalm 34:17 KJV

PrayerFather, receive the words and tears I cannot organize. Let Your listening presence become my first relief. Amen.

Exodus 15:13 KJV

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11:28 KJV

PrayerJesus, I bring You the weight I have normalized. Teach my body and soul to receive Your rest. Amen.

Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.

Matthew 4:19 KJV

PrayerJesus, loosen my grip on certainty. Give me courage to follow the light You have given today. Amen.

PrayerGood Shepherd, call me out of hiding. Help me live today as one fully known and still fully loved. Amen.
